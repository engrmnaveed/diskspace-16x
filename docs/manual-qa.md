# Manual macOS QA

Test on the oldest supported macOS version and a current version. Record the macOS version, CPU, filesystem, app build, and result for each run.

## Make a test folder

```sh
fixture="$(mktemp -d)"
mkdir -p "$fixture/Photos/Shoots" "$fixture/Archives"
dd if=/dev/urandom of="$fixture/Photos/Shoots/contact-sheet.bin" bs=1048576 count=8
dd if=/dev/urandom of="$fixture/Archives/small.bin" bs=1048576 count=1
ln "$fixture/Photos/Shoots/contact-sheet.bin" "$fixture/Photos/Shoots/linked-copy.bin"
ln -s "$fixture/Photos" "$fixture/shortcut"
mkfile -n 64m "$fixture/Archives/sparse.bin"
open "$fixture"
```

## Run the checks

- Open the fixture with the picker and then drop the folder from Finder. Repeat for a readable mounted local volume.
- Drill down through **Photos** and **Shoots**. Find the largest allocated file with the keyboard, reveal it in Finder, then repeat with the pointer. Copy its path and verify it matches the revealed item.
- Switch to **Largest Files**. Confirm the contact sheet comes first and the sparse file shows its actual allocated size. Compare the totals and paths with the CLI's allocated report.
- Switch between **List**, **Treemap**, and **Donut**. Check that chart tiles, slices, and shares follow allocated bytes within the current folder. Activate a folder or file from the chart.
- Navigate by breadcrumb, keyboard, and the app menus. Confirm the selected row, progress, issues, and actionable controls are legible with VoiceOver and in both appearances.
- Confirm the link alias and symbolic link never add to the bytes. Compare root totals with the CLI against the same fixture before it changes.
- Compare a few folder sizes with the on-disk figure in Finder's Get Info, which uses allocated bytes. The app and the CLI should show the same decimal values, such as `45.89 GB`. Hover a size in the list, the donut center, and a treemap tile, and confirm the tooltip shows the exact bytes and the GiB value. VoiceOver should read only the exact bytes. In the CLI explorer, open a file's details and confirm they show the same GiB value and byte count.
- Scan `/` on an APFS startup disk. In the app and CLI, confirm `/Users` is present and its contents do not reappear under `/System/Volumes/Data/Users`. Scan `/System/Volumes/Data` directly and confirm it includes its own `Users` folder.
- Add an unreadable descendant (`chmod 0`) as a standard user. Confirm the app keeps readable items and shows the folder as **Protected** with a lock. The notice should be neutral rather than an orange warning, and the sidebar should read "Protected items skipped". Scan Details should list the folder under Protected items. In the CLI, confirm `[protected]`, a one-line stderr summary, and exit code 2. Restore permissions after the check. Confirm an unreadable root fails with a clear message.
- Without Full Disk Access, scan `/` or your home folder. Confirm the neutral notice offers **Allow Full Disk Access…** and that it opens the Full Disk Access pane. Scan Details should list protected items grouped by containing folder, after any scan errors. Grant access to the build under test and return to the app. Confirm the notice offers **Scan Again**, and that the rescan no longer lists `~/Library/Containers`. Remove the grant afterwards if the test machine needs it off.
- Scan a large synthetic tree with files at several depths. Confirm a browseable two-level preview appears before deeper results. Drill into a pending folder and check that it fills in without losing the current location. Cancel from the progress strip and confirm the incomplete totals remain labeled. Close the window during another scan and verify the operation stops.
- Scan a test folder with local items and cloud-only entries. Compare placeholder availability before and after. Confirm cloud-only rows have zero allocated bytes and the contents of cloud-only directories are left unexpanded. If a known provider directory stalls, confirm other local branches finish and the skipped path appears in Scan Details.
- Click the **Name**, **Allocated**, and **Items** column headers in the folder list and in **Largest Files**. Confirm the sort indicator moves, a second click reverses the order, and the selected row stays selected. Check that **Photos** shows 3 items, counting the hard link once, and that the treemap and donut still rank by size.
- Choose **View → Size → Apparent Size**. Confirm the column header, the size summary, the shares, and the charts switch, and the sparse file now ranks first at 64 MB. Quit and reopen the app and confirm the choice persists. Compare with `space16x scan "$fixture" --apparent --depth 2`.
- In **Settings → Exclude from scans**, enter `Archives` and `*.bin` on separate lines, then `/` on a third line. Confirm the warning appears for `/` and a scan shows the error notice. Remove `/`, scan again, and confirm the header shows the excluded count, **Archives** is missing, and Scan Details explains the exclusion. Compare with `space16x scan "$fixture" --exclude Archives --exclude '*.bin'`. Clear the patterns afterwards.
- Verify scans do not modify or move the fixture items.
- Make a disposable copy of the fixture and scan it. Select two rows with ⌘-click, press ⌘⌫, and confirm the alert names the item count and allocated size. Click **Cancel** and confirm nothing moved. Press ⌘⌫ again, click **Move to Trash**, and confirm the rows disappear while the folder total, the parent's item count, and the charts drop by the same amount without a rescan. Check that the notice says space is freed when you empty the Trash. Open the Trash in Finder and confirm both items are there. Note whether Finder offers **Put Back** for them. Rescan and compare totals with `space16x scan`.
- Try **Move to Trash…** from a row's context menu, from the selection bar, and on a cloud-only item and the protected `chmod 0` folder. Confirm the last two stay put with a notice that explains why. Confirm the command is disabled while a scan runs.
- In another disposable copy, run `space16x` in Terminal. Mark a file and a folder with Space, press `d`, and confirm the footer names the count and size. Press `n`, then `d` and `y`. Confirm both rows leave and the totals drop. In Finder's Trash, choose **Put Back** for each item and confirm it returns to its folder. Press `r` in the explorer and confirm the items reappear with their sizes. Run `space16x --read-only` and confirm Space and `d` only show the read-only notice.
- Select a file and press Space. Confirm Quick Look opens, the arrow keys move the selection and the preview follows, and Space closes it. Try **Browse → Quick Look** (⌘Y) and the context menu. Select a folder and confirm Quick Look shows its summary. Select a cloud-only item and confirm Space shows a notice instead of downloading it.
- Open a folder of the fixture, add a large file to it in Terminal, and choose **Scan → Rescan Folder** (⌥⌘R). Confirm the progress strip names the folder, the new file appears, and the folder, the parent totals, the item counts, and the charts grow without a full rescan. The notice gives the new and earlier sizes. Delete the folder in Terminal and choose **Rescan Folder** from its row's context menu, then confirm it leaves the results. Start a rescan of a large folder and cancel it, and confirm the earlier results stay. Compare totals with `space16x scan`.
- Scan the fixture and choose **File → Export Scan…** (⇧⌘E). Confirm the suggested name ends in ` scan.json` and the notice reports the export. Run `space16x --import` on the file and compare the totals. Choose **File → Open Scan…** (⇧⌘O) and open the file. Confirm the sidebar says "Saved scan, read-only", the totals match the scan, and **Scan Again**, **Rescan Folder**, Quick Look, **Reveal in Finder**, and **Move to Trash** are disabled. Drop the JSON file on the window and confirm it opens the same way. Export from the CLI with `space16x scan "$fixture" --export cli.json` and open that too. Open a non-JSON file renamed to `.json` and confirm the notice explains it can't be read. Choose a folder afterwards and confirm a new scan turns the actions back on.
- On the start screen, confirm the volume list shows the startup disk and any mounted external or disk-image volume, with free space matching Finder's Get Info. Mount and eject a disk image and confirm the list updates. Click a volume and confirm the scan starts there. Compare the startup disk's figures with `space16x volumes`.
- Open **Settings** and confirm **Pro features** is off on a fresh install, and that no Compare button or menu item appears. Turn it on and off, quit, and confirm the choice persists.
- With **Pro features** off, confirm the start screen ends with one line, "More views and a menu-bar monitor are free in Pro." Click **Turn On in Settings…** and confirm Settings opens with the **Pro features** switch. Turn it on and confirm the line leaves. Turn it off, click the line's ×, quit and reopen, and confirm the line stays hidden while the switch in Settings still works.
- With **Pro features** on, export a scan of a disposable fixture copy. Grow one file, delete a folder, and add a new one in Terminal, then scan again. Click **Compare…** and choose the export. Confirm the **Change** column shows the growth in orange, **New** for the added folder, and a dash for unchanged items. Confirm the bar summarizes the folder's change and one removed item. Click the **Change** header twice and confirm it ranks the largest growth first and then the largest shrink. Drill into a folder and confirm the column follows. Click the × and confirm the column leaves. Compare totals with `space16x diff export.json "$fixture"` from a Pro CLI. Turn Pro features off while comparing and confirm Compare ends.
- With **Pro features** on, scan a disposable fixture where `touch -t 202001010000 old.bin` dated one file in a folder of newer files. Confirm the **Modified** column shows each folder's newest file date and the old file's 2020 date, and a dash for an empty folder. Click the **Modified** header and confirm the newest items come first, then the oldest. Switch to **Largest Files** and confirm the column follows. Move the newest file in a folder to the Trash and confirm the folder's date falls back. Turn Pro features off and confirm the column leaves and the list sorts by size. Compare dates with the explorer of a Pro CLI at 100 columns and with `space16x scan "$fixture" --json`, whose `newestModified` values are Unix seconds.
- With **Pro features** on, confirm **Types** appears in the Contents picker and **Browse → File Types** (⌘5), and that both are gone with Pro features off. In a fixture with `a.MOV`, `b.mov`, a hard link to `a.MOV`, `Makefile` and `.bashrc`, choose **Types** and confirm `.mov` leads with 2 files, and **No extension** has 2. Open a subfolder and confirm the list follows it. Switch **View → Size** and confirm the sizes and ranking follow. Move a file to the Trash and confirm its type's row updates. Turn Pro features off while on Types and confirm the list view returns. Compare with `space16x scan "$fixture" --types 10` and `t` in a Pro CLI explorer.
- With **Pro features** on, scan a disposable fixture and confirm `~/Library/Application Support/Space 16X/History` doesn't exist yet. Choose **Save to History** from **Compare…** and confirm the notice, then **Scan → Show History in Finder** shows the folder with one `.json` file and `index.jsonl`, readable only by you. Grow a file in Terminal, scan again, open **Compare…** and confirm the saved scan is listed with its date and size. Choose it and confirm the **Change** column and the bar name the date. Confirm **Scan → Compare with History** lists the same scan and is disabled for a folder without saved scans. Turn Pro features off and confirm the history items leave the menus. Compare with `space16x diff "$HOME/Library/Application Support/Space 16X/History/<file>.json" "$fixture"` from a Pro CLI.
- With **Pro features** on, choose **Scan → Explain Disk Space** (⇧⌘X) and confirm the sheet names the startup volume, its size, used and free space match `space16x explain /` from a Pro CLI, **Purgeable** appears, and the APFS volume list includes the data volume, Preboot and VM. Right-click a volume on the start screen and choose **Explain Disk Space**. Create a Time Machine local snapshot with `tmutil localsnapshot` if the machine allows it and confirm the snapshot count appears with one line per snapshot date, oldest first, and the note that macOS removes each after about 24 hours, matching `space16x explain /`. Turn Pro features off and confirm both commands leave.
- With **Pro features** on, scan a disposable folder holding `site/node_modules` (over 1 MB), `app/Cargo.toml` with `app/target` (over 1 MB) and a `target` folder without `Cargo.toml`. Choose **Cleanup** in the sidebar (⌘6) and confirm it lists `target` (**Rust build output**) and `node_modules` (**Node packages**) with their folders, but not the other `target`. Select both, press ⌘⌫, confirm, and check that both rows leave and the totals drop. Compare with `space16x scan "$fixture" --cleanup` and `u` in a Pro CLI explorer. Turn Pro features off while on Cleanup and confirm Folders returns.
- With **Pro features** on, scan a disposable fixture holding `Library/Developer/Xcode/Archives/App.xcarchive/app` (2 MB) and a `node_modules` folder (2 MB). Choose **Cleanup** and confirm **Archives** says "Xcode archives · review, can't be recreated", isn't selected, and the caption's total leaves it out with "1 to review". Select it, press ⌘⌫ and confirm the confirmation starts with "Xcode archives can't be recreated." Cancel. Select `node_modules` and confirm no such warning. Compare with `space16x scan "$fixture" --cleanup` and `u` in a Pro CLI explorer, whose prompt gives the same warning.
- With **Pro features** on, make an APFS clone with `cp -c big.bin clone.bin` in a disposable folder, select `big.bin` and press ⌘⌫. Confirm the alert says emptying the Trash frees at least 0 bytes or a small figure because clones share the rest. Click **Cancel**. Select a file without clones and confirm the alert keeps the usual wording. Compare with `space16x scan "$fixture" --exclusive`.
- With **Pro features** on, scan a disposable folder with two identical 2 MB files and a third that differs in its last byte. Choose **Duplicates** in the sidebar (⌘7) and confirm nothing is read until you click **Find Duplicates**, then that the two copies appear as **Copy 1 of 2** and **Copy 2 of 2** and the caption gives the extra space. Move one copy to the Trash and confirm the group leaves. Scan again and confirm the list asks to find duplicates again. Compare with `space16x duplicates "$fixture"`.
- With **Pro features** on, in a disposable folder on APFS, make `a.bin` (2 MB), a separate copy `b.bin` (`cp a.bin b.bin`) and a clone `c.bin` (`cp -c a.bin c.bin`). Find Duplicates and confirm `a.bin` and `c.bin` say **clone, frees nothing**, `b.bin` doesn't, and the caption gives 2.0 MB in extra copies, not 4.0 MB. `space16x duplicates` gives the same Extra and marks the same two files **(clone)**.
- With **Pro features** on, scan a disposable APFS folder holding `a.bin` (2 MB), its clone `cp -c a.bin c.bin` and a hard link to a third file. Choose **View → Show Exclusive Size** and confirm the caption shows "Measuring exclusive sizes" with a percentage, then an **Exclusive** column where `a.bin` and `c.bin` show less than their allocated size and the linked file shows zero. Click the **Exclusive** header twice and confirm it ranks most exclusive first, then least. Move a file to the Trash and confirm the column measures again. Open a saved scan and confirm the caption says exclusive sizes need a scan. Turn the menu item off and confirm the column leaves. Compare with `X` in a Pro CLI explorer and `space16x scan "$fixture" --exclusive`.
- With **Pro features** on, turn on **Settings → Pro features → Show free space in the menu bar**. Confirm the menu-bar item shows the startup disk's free space, its menu lists each volume, **Open Space 16X** brings the window back, and **Hide from Menu Bar** removes the item and turns the setting off. Set **Notify when free space drops below** above the current free space and confirm one notification appears within a minute and doesn't repeat. Turn Pro features off and confirm the item leaves. Check in Activity Monitor that the app does no periodic work with the item off.
- With **Pro features** on, compare a scan with a saved one, then choose **Scan → Save Footprint Receipt…** or the receipt button beside the comparison. Enter "first build" and save. Open the Markdown file and confirm the title, both scan times, the totals, the files gained and lost, and the largest changes with paths relative to the scanned folder. Confirm the command is disabled when not comparing.
- With **Pro features** on, while comparing, open **Scan → Save Footprint Receipt…**, name the operation "build" and type `abc` as the budget: confirm the hint turns orange and **Save…** is disabled. Type a budget smaller than the folder's growth, save, and confirm the receipt says **Over budget** with the amount over. Open the sheet again, type "build", and confirm the budget fills in. Save with a larger budget and confirm **Within budget** with the amount to spare. Compare with `space16x diff "$export" "$fixture" --budget SIZE`, whose exit status is `3` when over.
- With **Pro features** on, choose **File → New Window** (⌘N). Scan different folders in the two windows and confirm each keeps its own results, and that ⌘R, ⌘⌫ and **Browse** commands act on the window in front. Close every window, open only **Settings**, and confirm the scan and browse commands are disabled. Turn Pro features off and confirm **New Window** leaves the File menu.
- With a Pro CLI, run `space16x scan "$fixture" --html report.html` and open the page in a browser. Confirm the treemap, contents, largest files and file types, that it follows light and dark mode, and that it loads nothing from the network. Press `m` in the explorer and confirm the treemap draws the open folder, the arrow keys move the highlight, and `m` returns to the list.

Clean up the temporary folder when finished.
